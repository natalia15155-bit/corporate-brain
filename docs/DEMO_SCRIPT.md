# Corporate Brain — demo script

Target duration: 2 minutes 35 seconds. Maximum allowed recording: 3 minutes.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

**Narration**

> Companies do not just forget decisions. They lose the connection between a
> decision, the evidence behind it, and the changes that can make it unsafe.
> Corporate Brain turns governed DataHub context into decisions people can
> verify, approve, and remember.

**Screen**

- Open Corporate Brain.
- Keep the runtime status visible long enough to show whether DataHub is live.

## 0:20–0:45 — Start from the user's role

**Narration**

> I am a data analyst preparing an Ecommerce control dashboard. Corporate Brain
> first shows only the assets my role is allowed to use, including owners,
> freshness, quality, sensitivity, and source type.

**Screen**

- Select the Data Analyst profile.
- Show the permitted domains.
- Scan the Data Catalog.

## 0:45–1:20 — Check operational readiness

**Narration**

> Instead of returning a generic chat answer, Corporate Brain checks the exact
> capabilities required for the outcome: product identity, inventory, sales
> velocity, complaints, alert routing, and their lineage. Each requirement is
> marked Ready, Missing, or Blocked using deterministic rules.

**Screen**

- Select the Ecommerce dashboard readiness starter.
- Submit the question.
- Show the seven capability results.
- Expand one supporting source and one lineage connection.

## 1:20–1:50 — Turn evidence into action

**Narration**

> The evidence supports two proposed controls: an early stockout warning for
> star products and a critical product-complaint alert routed to CX and Product.
> The thresholds are visible and editable; no alert or shared memory is created
> silently.

**Screen**

- Show both alert cards.
- Adjust one threshold.
- Show risks or missing evidence if present.

## 1:50–2:20 — Human-confirmed DataHub write-back

**Narration**

> When I approve the result, the server revalidates the request, recomputes the
> Decision Card, and writes a bounded decision document back through DataHub MCP.
> It links the decision to the governed assets by their DataHub URNs.

**Screen**

- Confirm the save action.
- Show the successful live DataHub destination.
- Open DataHub and show the saved decision document with one related asset.

## 2:20–2:35 — Why it matters

**Narration**

> Corporate Brain is not another chatbot over company data. DataHub provides the
> ownership, lineage, quality, and access context that lets the agent decide when
> it can help — and when it must abstain.

**Screen**

- Finish on the Decision Brief with evidence and trust status visible.
